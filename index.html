About Me

I am an educator and science engagement specialist turned aspiring software developer, passionate about bridging the gap between education and technology. With over five years of experience leading STEM programmes, from managing outreach at SANSA to directing academic initiatives at Somerset College, I have built a career on empowering learners through hands-on science and coding experiences.

I am currently studying Frontend Software Development at CodeSpace Academy in Cape Town. My focus is on creating clean, responsive websites using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a growing interest in building scalable EdTech solutions for education access in Africa.
